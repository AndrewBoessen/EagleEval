Welcome Back Eagles 🦅
Updates to Eagle Eval

As of May 2026, the creators of Eagle Eval, Sebastian Pucher and Andrew Boessen, graduated from Boston College. With the closing of this chapter, we wanted to extend a sincere thank you to all the students, professors, faculty, and staff who have supported us, provided feedback, and genuinely believed in our platform and the impact it’s had on campus. We are forever thankful to the BC community for not only shaping Eagle Eval into what it is, but also for challenging us and helping us grow as people along the way.

Believe it or not, we built Eagle Eval all the way back in 2023 as sophomores in Roncalli (roncastle). Little did we know that our year on CoRo would be the beginning of something so meaningful to students. It’s allowed us to genuinely help our peers, push the envelope on what the school allows, and honestly just have a ton of fun in the process. Because of this, we chose to self fund the project entirely out of pocket because we really believed in what we were doing.

While we are moving on, the site isn't disappearing completely. Eagle Eval will remain live as an archive so you can view lifetime statistics and example Class and Professor pages.

In reflecting on our time at BC as a whole as well as our journey with Eagle Eval, it's a reminder that you don’t need permission to start creating something. So if you’re some sophomore in Roncalli reading this right now (or freshman, junior, or senior), just know that you can shape your surroundings, you can make a difference for good, you can build, learn, grow, fail, try again, and most importantly, contribute something that is bigger than yourself.

As for us, we understand that it’s time to start over again … to feel the sadness and excitement that comes with saying goodbye to something, and starting new. To know that college is over and that Eagle Eval is just one chapter in our lives as builders… and to get absolutely stoked about what the future holds.

Good luck on the rest of your BC journey. I know that Andrew and I are so excited to see what the next generation of BC students do … we’ll be rooting for you.

Signing off

Sebastian Pucher & Andrew Boessen
